GREEN SPROUT - SERVICE REQUEST & MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
===================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------
Green Sprout is a multi-role enterprise service management platform designed to streamline customer support, technician dispatching, and service audit workflows. It follows a modular "Clean Architecture" for scalability and maintainability.

2. USER ROLES & PERSONAS
------------------------
* ADMIN: Global oversight, dealer management, and high-level reporting.
* DEALER: Regional management, technician assignment, and branch delegation.
* CUSTOMER: Complaint registration, real-time tracking, and service history.
* TECHNICIAN (SERVICE PERSON): Task execution (Remote/Visit), proof-of-work submission, and status updates.

3. CORE FEATURES BY MODULE
--------------------------

A. AUTHENTICATION & ONBOARDING
- Secure Login via OTP (One-Time Password).
- Role-based Profile Setup and Routing.
- Persistent Session Management (Token-based).

B. CUSTOMER PORTAL
- Dashboard Overview: Real-time summary of active and completed tickets.
- Complaint Management:
  * Easy-to-use "Raise Complaint" interface with issue categorization.
  * Real-time ticket tracking with detailed status updates.
- Service Reports: View and download service history and status reports.

C. DEALER MANAGEMENT (DEALER PORTAL)
- Operations Dashboard: Statistics on team performance and ticket resolution.
- Resource Management:
  * Add and manage Sub-Dealers and Technicians.
  * Delegate branches and service areas.
- Technician Assignment: Smart routing of customer tickets to field staff.
- Service Monitoring: Track technician progress through a milestone-based stepper.

D. TECHNICIAN PORTAL (SERVICE PERSON)
- Task Management: Dedicated view for assigned service requests.
- Service Execution:
  * Support Modes: Toggle between "Site Visit" and "Remote Support".
  * Milestone Stepper: Step-by-step progress tracking for transparency.
- Communication: Quick-action buttons to call or chat with customers.
- Completion Workflow: Diagnosis notes and proof-of-work (photo) uploads.

E. ADMIN CONTROL CENTER
- Global Analytics: Financial and operational KPIs using interactive charts.
- Dealer Hierarchy: Management of the entire dealer network.
- Audit Timeline: Detailed logs of every action taken on a ticket for accountability.
- Ticket Sidebar: Quick-access navigation through the global ticket backlog.

4. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
---------------------------
- Framework: Flutter (Cross-platform Android/iOS).
- State Management: Flutter BLoC / Cubit for reactive UI.
- Navigation: GoRouter for deep-linking and hierarchical routing.
- Dependency Injection: GetIt for service locator pattern.
- Data Visualization: FL Chart for analytics and reports.
- UI/UX: Custom Material 3 theme, Rive/Lottie animations, and SVG support.

5. COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES
-------------------------
- Multi-tier Hierarchy: Supports complex business structures (Admin -> Dealer -> Sub-Dealer).
- Hybrid Support: Built-in support for both physical visits and remote troubleshooting.
- Audit Ready: Detailed timeline logging ensures transparency for insurance or quality audits.
- Scalable Architecture: Feature-first structure allows adding new roles or services easily.
